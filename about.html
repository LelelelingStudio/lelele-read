关于了了了读 About lelele Read

「了了了读」是了了了岭工作室旗下的在线阅读产品。我们致力于打造一个轻量、干净、无广告的网页阅读器，让你在繁杂的互联网中拥有一片纯净的阅读空间。 'lelele Read' is an online reading product under Leleleling Studio. We are dedicated to creating a lightweight, clean, and ad-free web reader, providing you with a pure reading space amidst the complex internet.

为什么做「了了了读」？ Why 'lelele Read'?

我们发现在网上找一本排版舒服、没有弹窗广告、不需要下载臃肿 App 的电子书越来越难。于是我们决定用最纯粹的 Web 技术（HTML/CSS/JS），把经典的公版书籍、或者是我们自己创作的内容，重新进行精美的排版，直接呈现给每一位读者。 We found it increasingly difficult to find e-books online with comfortable typography, no pop-up ads, and no need to download bloated apps. So, we decided to use the purest web technologies (HTML/CSS/JS) to beautifully typeset classic public domain books or our own creations and present them directly to every reader.

关于工作室 About the Studio

了了了岭工作室（Leleleling Studio）由一群热爱技术和创作的开发者组成。我们既做像「了了了游」这样的游戏分享平台，也做像「了了了读」这样纯粹的阅读工具。我们相信，好的工具应该服务于内容，而不是喧宾夺主。 Leleleling Studio is composed of developers who love technology and creation. We build game sharing platforms like 'lelele Game' and pure reading tools like 'lelele Read'. We believe that good tools should serve the content, not overshadow it.
