版权与侵权投诉指引

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 25 日

「了了了读」高度重视知识产权保护。若您认为本平台展示的某部书籍（尤其是共创书库中的投稿作品）侵犯了您的合法权益，请按照本指引向我们提交正式的侵权通知。

一、通知要求

根据《中华人民共和国民法典》第 1195 条及相关法律法规，您的侵权通知应当包含以下内容：

  • 权利人身份证明：包括您的姓名（或机构名称）、身份证/营业执照复印件、联系方式（电话、邮箱）。
  • 权属证明：包括但不限于著作权登记证书、首发证明、原创底稿等能够证明您拥有相关权利的材料。
  • 侵权内容定位：请明确指出涉嫌侵权的书籍名称、作者，以及该书籍在本平台的具体链接（URL）。
  • 真实性声明：请声明您通知中的陈述真实、准确，并愿意承担因不实通知产生的法律责任。
⚠️ 注意：若您发出的通知不完整或不符合法定要求，本平台将暂缓处理。因错误通知导致平台或投稿者遭受损失的，权利人应依法承担赔偿责任。

二、提交方式

请将上述材料打包发送至本平台指定邮箱：

leleleling@2980.com

邮件标题请注明：【侵权投诉】+ 书籍名称 + 您的姓名/机构名称。

三、平台处理流程

  • 审核：我们在收到符合要求的通知后，将在 2 个工作日内进行初步审核。
  • 转通知：审核通过后，我们将立即将您的通知转送给被投诉内容的上传者。
  • 下架处理：依据《民法典》规定，我们将及时采取删除、屏蔽、断开链接等必要措施。
  • 反通知：若投稿者提交了合规的反通知，我们将把反通知转送给您。若您未在合理期限内提起诉讼，我们将恢复被下架的内容。

Copyright Infringement Notice Guidelines

Last updated: October 25, 2026

'lelele Read' highly values intellectual property protection. If you believe that a book displayed on the Platform (especially submitted works in the Community Library) infringes your rights, please submit a formal infringement notice according to these guidelines.

I. Notice Requirements

According to Article 1195 of the Civil Code of the PRC and relevant laws, your notice must contain:

  • Proof of Identity: Your name (or organization name), ID/Business License copy, and contact info (phone, email).
  • Proof of Rights: Copyright registration certificates, proof of first publication, original drafts, etc.
  • Location of Infringing Content: The specific name, author, and exact URL of the infringing book on our Platform.
  • Statement of Truthfulness: A statement that the information in your notice is accurate, and you are authorized to act on behalf of the rights holder.
⚠️ Warning: If your notice is incomplete or non-compliant, the Platform will postpone processing. The rights holder shall bear legal liability for damages caused by false notices.

II. How to Submit

Please pack the above materials and send them to our designated email:

leleleling@2980.com

Please use the subject: [Infringement Notice] + Book Name + Your Name/Organization.

III. Platform Processing Procedure

  • Review: We will conduct an initial review within 2 business days of receiving a compliant notice.
  • Forwarding: If approved, we will immediately forward your notice to the uploader of the content.
  • Takedown: In accordance with the Civil Code, we will promptly remove, block, or disable access to the content.
  • Counter-Notice: If the uploader submits a compliant counter-notice, we will forward it to you. If you do not file a lawsuit within a reasonable period, we will restore the content.
